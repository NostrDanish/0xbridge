Privacy-first Nostr bridge by 0xPrivacy. No email. No accounts. Your npub is your identity.

Send a message to the bridge

Messages are delivered as NIP-17 gift-wrapped DMs. The bridge reads them, parses idea: lines and links, and replies via DM. Nothing is stored on a server — only on the relay, ephemerally.

Admission

This bridge is free to use. For relay access (NosFlare compute), the bridge replies with payment or details via DM. No email, no forms — just a Nostr DM back to you.

How it works

1. This site is hosted as an nsite (NIP-5A) — static files on Blossom, served from the bridge npub subdomain.
2. Your message is encrypted client-side with NIP-44 and sent as a gift-wrapped DM.
3. The bridge bot decrypts, logs the signal, and replies.
4. No backend, no tracking, no vendor lock-in.
